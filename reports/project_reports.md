# Customer Behavior Analysis Project Report

## 1. Introduction

In today’s data-driven environment, businesses collect large volumes of customer data but often fail to convert it into actionable insights. Understanding customer purchasing behavior is critical for improving revenue, optimizing marketing strategies, and enhancing customer experience.

This project focuses on analyzing customer shopping behavior using data analytics techniques to identify patterns, trends, and key business insights.

---

## 2. Objectives

- To analyze customer purchasing patterns  
- To identify key revenue contributors  
- To segment customers based on their purchase behavior  
- To evaluate the impact of discounts and subscription models  
- To generate insights for better business decision-making  

---

## 3. Dataset Description

The dataset used in this project contains information related to customer transactions and behavior. The key attributes include:

- Customer Demographics: Age, Gender, Location  
- Purchase Details: Item Purchased, Category, Purchase Amount  
- Behavioral Metrics: Previous Purchases, Frequency of Purchases  
- Marketing Factors: Discount Applied, Subscription Status  
- Customer Feedback: Review Rating  

---

## 4. Data Preprocessing

### 4.1 Data Cleaning

- Missing values in the review_rating column were handled using median imputation grouped by category  
- The promo_code_used column was removed as it contained redundant information  
- Column names were standardized to lowercase and formatted using underscores  

### 4.2 Feature Engineering

- Age Group Segmentation:
  - Young Adult  
  - Adult  
  - Middle-aged  
  - Senior  

- Purchase Frequency Conversion:
  - Converted categorical frequency into numerical values (days)  

- Additional structured fields were created for better segmentation and querying  

---

## 5. Database Integration

The cleaned dataset was integrated into SQL Server for efficient querying and analysis.

- Tools Used:
  - SQLAlchemy  
  - pyodbc  

- The dataset was stored in a table named:

  customer  

This enabled the use of SQL queries for solving business problems.

---

## 6. Data Analysis Using SQL

### 6.1 Revenue Analysis

- Total revenue generated by gender  
- Revenue contribution by different age groups  

### 6.2 Customer Behavior Analysis

- Identification of customers who used discounts but still spent above average  
- Analysis of repeat buyers and their subscription behavior  

### 6.3 Product Analysis

- Top 5 products with the highest average review ratings  
- Most frequently purchased products within each category  
- Products with the highest discount usage  

### 6.4 Operational Insights

- Comparison of average purchase amounts between Standard and Express shipping  

### 6.5 Customer Segmentation

Customers were categorized into:

- New Customers (1 previous purchase)  
- Returning Customers (2–10 purchases)  
- Loyal Customers (more than 10 purchases)

---

## 8. Key Insights

- Loyal customers contribute the highest share of revenue  
- Young adults generate the maximum number of purchases  
- The clothing category drives the highest sales and revenue  
- Subscription users tend to spend more compared to non-subscribers  
- Discounts influence purchasing behavior but do not always reduce spending
